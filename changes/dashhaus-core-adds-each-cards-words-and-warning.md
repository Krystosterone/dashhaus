`@dashhaus/core` adds each card's words and warning level beside its model, so a skin of your own says what Orb and Field say: `cover/words`, `alarm/words`, `lock/words`, `light/words`, `climate/words`, `toggle/words` and `sound/words`, a `severity` entry for the cover, sensor, weather, Owlet and alarm, `WARNING_WORD_MAX`, the battery's `batteryText`, the guest Wi-Fi's `noneWord` and `NONE_HINT`, and `rangeSummary` and `nowText` for a reading's day and its figure now.
