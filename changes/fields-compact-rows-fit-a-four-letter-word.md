Field's compact rows fit a four-letter word on their screen (LOCK, OPEN, DOCK, HOLD) at every width, and a hot Fahrenheit day (104.3°F) no longer shrinks on a narrow row. A module's Today line and its fader's legend stay on one line on narrow modules, ending in an ellipsis where they must, instead of wrapping onto the hour strip or the fader's slot.
