A media player card in Orb and Field: what is playing with its artwork, how far in (a line and a clock that run on by themselves), and one press to pause, play or turn it on, with skip, volume, mute, the source and power in its details; media_player entities join both skins' automatic layouts with the controls. @dashhaus/core adds useHaMediaPlayer, HaMediaPlayer, the media player model, words and seeds, usePlayhead in @dashhaus/core/hooks, and inWords and fmtPlayTime in @dashhaus/core/entities/format.
