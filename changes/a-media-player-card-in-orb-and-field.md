A media player card in Orb and Field: what is playing with its artwork, how far in (a line and a clock that run on by themselves and stop at the end, or none on a dashboard set `quiet`), and one press to pause, play or turn it on, lit while it plays, buffers or is on, with skip, volume, mute, the source and power in its details; media_player entities join both skins' automatic layouts with the controls. Field's sound module now says why its volume is unlit to a screen reader too ("62 percent, sound off"). @dashhaus/core adds useHaMediaPlayer, HaMediaPlayer, the media player model, words (with isActive, volumeReason, SKIP_WORDS and artworkLabel) and seeds, @dashhaus/core/entities/volume for any card's volume in words (volumeText, volumeSaid), the sound's soundVolumeReason, usePlayhead in @dashhaus/core/hooks, and inWords and fmtPlayTime in @dashhaus/core/entities/format.
