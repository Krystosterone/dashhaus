A binary sensor card in Orb and Field: a door, a window, a leak or smoke in its device class's words, with its battery; smoke, gas, carbon monoxide and leaks are marked as warnings, and the automatic layouts add every door, window, leak and smoke sensor (Orb's under Security). A binary sensor in YAML takes `battery`, and `@dashhaus/core/lovelace/layout` adds `safetySensors` for a skin's own automatic layout.
