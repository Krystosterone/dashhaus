A garage door, door or gate that reports a position can now be moved part way in both skins, and it asks first: a tap on Orb's bar or Field's fader asks straight away, a drag asks when you let go ("Move Front gate to 40%?"), and Cancel puts it back. Its quick positions are Closed and Open. `@dashhaus/core` adds the words for it (`coverQuestion` and `coverAskAction` take a position, `levelQuestion` and `levelAction` for any level that asks).
