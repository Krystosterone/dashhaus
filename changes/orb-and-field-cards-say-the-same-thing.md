Orb and Field cards say the same thing in the same words: every warning fits a 13-character row (an Owlet's alerts, "Stopped 11:49" once its readings stop, the weather's "Strong gusts", the alarm's "Entry delay · Front door"), the small print says when first and then what the device is, Field says "Last seen" while unavailable, and a charging Owlet sock says Charging and no longer warns of its battery. Orb's triggered alarm fills its slot with the shared alarm pill and Disarm, and its entry delay, a lock's battery and the weather's figures no longer repeat the warning in orange. Code that matches `@dashhaus/core`'s words gets the new ones: the Owlet's `ALERT_WORD` ("Critical O₂", "Low pulse", "Sock offline", "No base power", "Battery dying"), the weather's "Strong gusts" in place of "Gusts 84 km/h" (`weatherWarningsSaid` still says the speed), and `ARMED_LABEL.custom_bypass` "Armed custom".
