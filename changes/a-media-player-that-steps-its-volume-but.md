A media player that steps its volume but can't set it, such as a TV over HDMI-CEC, gets Down and Up in its details: two pills in Orb's sheet and a minus and a plus key in Field's panel, each one step. Core adds onVolumeUp and onVolumeDown to the media player's actions and supportsVolumeStep to its entity; Orb's TogglePill gains a disabled state.
