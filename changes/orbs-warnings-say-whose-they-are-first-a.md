Orb's warnings say whose they are first: a screen reader hears "Warning: Porch: Battery 12%, replace soon" or "Alert: Hallway smoke: Smoke detected" on every card, in every size and in the details. @dashhaus/core/entities/binarySensor/words adds alertSaid and @dashhaus/core/entities/vacuum/words adds vacuumWarningSaid, a warning said without the card's name, for a skin that puts the name first itself.
