A vacuum card in Orb and Field: a robot vacuum (`vacuum.*`) said in Home Assistant's activities (Cleaning, Docked, Returning, Paused, Idle, Error), integrations' names for them mapped and any other state in its own words, with one press that pauses it while it moves and starts or resumes it while it stands, sending it to its dock or locating it from its details, and its fan speeds there too. An error is marked as an urgent warning with what went wrong, a low battery as advisory while it stands away from the dock and isn't charging (not while it cleans or heads home), and the automatic layouts place it with the controls by area. `@dashhaus/core` adds `useHaVacuum`, `HaVacuum`, `vacuumFromHass`, `createVacuumHandlers` and `VACUUM_ACTIONS` (start, pause, return to base and locate, each gated on `supported_features`, and the fan speed as a choice), `vacuumHistoryFromHass`, and `@dashhaus/core/entities/vacuum/words` and `…/severity` for its words (`vacuumWord`, `vacuumPress`, `vacuumWarning`) and severity (`vacuumSeverity`).
