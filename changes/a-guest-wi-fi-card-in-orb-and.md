A guest Wi-Fi card in Orb and Field: the guest network's name, what a guest types to use it, and the network's join QR code, with Refresh and New code in its details. Lovelace picks it for any UniFi Hotspot Manager `sensor.*_voucher`; with `card: guestWifi` it also shows a Wi-Fi password kept in an `input_text`, `text` or `sensor`, its actions a `button`, `input_button` or `script`, and New password asks first.
