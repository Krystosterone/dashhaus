One `cover.*` entity: curtains, blinds, shades, shutters, awnings, windows, garage doors, doors and gates.

## Quick example

<Example file="entities/cover/CoverButtons.tsx" />

Open and close work on every cover. Stop, a position and a tilt depend on what the cover reports.

A garage door, a door or a gate lets a person in, so it must never move on one stray press: a tap brushed across a
wall panel could open the house, or close a door on someone. Both skins ask first for these three, as they do before
unlocking a lock: a press opens the details on the question (`Close Garage door?`, what it means, then Cancel and
`Close garage door`). Stop is always one press, and their position is shown, never set. `coverAsksFirst` says which
covers ask, and the question's words are core's, so your own controls can ask the same way, as the example does.

A garage door Home Assistant gives no `device_class` (a template cover, say) is drawn as a shade and moves on one
press. Give it its class, `garage`, `door` or `gate`, in the cover's settings or under `customize` in Home Assistant,
and it asks first. Or give it the class on the dashboard alone: in Orb's or Field's YAML, add `device_class` to the
cover's card, and it is drawn and guarded as one, whatever Home Assistant says.

## Component

`<HaCover>` is `useHaCover` as a component that draws nothing of its own. It takes every option of the hook as a
prop, and hands the hook's result to its child function. Use it where hooks can't go, such as one per item of a list.

<ApiMembers from="@dashhaus/core" name="HaCover" only={["entityId", "children"]} />

## Hook

<ApiSignature from="@dashhaus/core" name="useHaCover" />

It reads the cover, its area and today's history. `deviceClass` is Home Assistant's `device_class`, so you can draw a garage door differently from a curtain.

#### `actions`: CoverHandlers

<ApiMembers from="@dashhaus/core" name="CoverHandlers" />

Every action on an unavailable entity is reported to `onActionError` as `unsupported` and never sent. A cover Home Assistant reports as `unknown` is not unavailable: its state is `"unknown"`, and every action is sent.

## Every cover

Hooks can't be called in a loop, so a list renders one headless component per entity. The selector returns a string: `useHaSelector` re-renders only when its value changes, and a string of ids changes only when an entity comes or goes. Lists skip each item's history read with `loadHistory={false}`.
