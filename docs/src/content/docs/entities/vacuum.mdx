One `vacuum.*` entity, a robot vacuum: whether it is cleaning, docked, on its way back, paused, idle or stopped with an
error; its battery and fan speed, which it reports itself; and what it can do, from what it declares to Home
Assistant.

## Quick example

<Example file="entities/vacuum/StartPause.tsx" />

Integrations name the same activity differently: one says `charging` where Home Assistant says `docked`, another
`returning_home` or `stopped`. They are mapped onto Home Assistant's own, a kind of cleaning named its own way
(`spot_cleaning`, `mopping`) is cleaning, and any other state is said in its own words, `Emptying the bin`, never
guessed. An error is a warning; so is a low battery while it stands away from its dock, not cleaning, not on its way home and not charging.

## Component

`<HaVacuum>` is `useHaVacuum` as a component that draws nothing of its own. It takes every option of the hook as a prop,
and hands the hook's result to its child function. Use it where hooks can't go, such as one per item of a list.

## Hook

<ApiSignature from="@dashhaus/core" name="useHaVacuum" />

It reads the vacuum, its area and today's cleaning. The battery is the vacuum's own, so there is no companion to pass.

### Returns

#### `entity`: VacuumEntity

Each `supports…` flag comes from what the vacuum declares to Home Assistant. Its battery, status and fan speed are known
only while it is available.

<ApiMembers from="@dashhaus/core/entities/vacuum/types" name="VacuumEntity" />

#### `history`: VacuumHistory

Today, from Home Assistant's recorder, kept up to date by every change since: the hours it was cleaning. Pass `history`
to supply your own, or `loadHistory={false}` to skip the read.

<ApiMembers from="@dashhaus/core/entities/vacuum/types" name="VacuumHistory" />

#### `actions`: VacuumHandlers

<ApiMembers from="@dashhaus/core" name="VacuumHandlers" />

An action the vacuum doesn't declare, or any action while it is unavailable, is reported to `onActionError` as
`unsupported` and never sent; a fan speed it doesn't list is reported as `invalid_input`.

## In a skin

Orb's `HaVacuumTile` and Field's `HaVacuumModule` are `useHaVacuum` above each skin's card. One press pauses it while it
moves and starts it while it stands; returning to its dock, locating it and its fan speeds are in its details. An error
is marked as a warning, never a glow. They take the hook's options as props, plus their own:
