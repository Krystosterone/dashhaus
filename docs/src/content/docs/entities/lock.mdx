One `lock.*` entity, with the battery sensor on its device: whether it is locked, on its way or jammed, who or what
changed it last, whether it wants a code, and whether it can unlatch the door too.

## Quick example

<Example file="entities/lock/UnlockWithCode.tsx" />

Locking is the safe direction, so it can take one press. Unlocking never should: a stray tap, a child or a pet must
not open a door, so both skins ask first and take the code when the lock wants one. Build your own controls the same
way.

The handlers never reject. Each answers `"done"` when Home Assistant took the call, `"wrong_code"` when the code was
refused or can't fit the lock's pattern, and `"failed"` when the call didn't go through; a failure also goes to
`onActionError`.

## Component

`<HaLock>` is `useHaLock` as a component that draws nothing of its own. It takes every option of the hook as a prop,
and hands the hook's result to its child function. Use it where hooks can't go, such as one per item of a list.

## Hook

<ApiSignature from="@dashhaus/core" name="useHaLock" />

It reads the lock, its area and, given `batteryEntityId`, the battery sensor on the same device. A lock shows its last
change, not a day's history, so there is no history read and no `history` in the result.

## In a skin

Orb's `HaLockTile` and Field's `HaLockModule` are `useHaLock` above each skin's card. A press on a locked lock opens
its details on the question, or on the keypad; Cancel takes the focus, so Enter never unlocks. A jam is marked as a
warning, never a glow. They take the hook's options as props, plus their own:
