Home Assistant's `sun.sun`, from its Sun integration: whether the sun is up, and when it next sets, rises, dawns,
dusks and peaks. With your home's location, today's path of the sun too. Read-only.

## Quick example

<Example file="entities/sun/NextEvent.tsx" />

`sunNext` is what a card leads with: the sunset while the sun is up, the sunrise while it is down. `sunWhen` counts down
to it from the entity's own minute: `Sets in 4h 35m`, `Rises in 9m`, or its day when it is more than a day off
(`Rises Thu`). Where neither comes within two days, as in a polar summer or winter, `sunAllDay` says `Up all day` or
`Down all day`. Times are on the 24-hour clock (`20:30`). Every skin says it in these words.

## Component

`<HaSun>` is `useHaSun` as a component that draws nothing of its own. It takes every option of the hook as a prop, and
hands the hook's result to its child function. Use it where hooks can't go, such as one per item of a list.

<ApiMembers from="@dashhaus/core" name="HaSun" only={["entityId", "children"]} />

## Hook

<ApiSignature from="@dashhaus/core" name="useHaSun" />

It reads the sun and counts from a clock that turns each minute. Today's path is worked out from your home's latitude
and longitude, read once from Home Assistant's configuration; pass `latitude` and `longitude` to draw it for another
place, or `loadHistory: false` to leave it out.

#### `history`: SunHistory

Today's path of the sun from midnight to midnight, worked out from the location rather than read from Home Assistant's
recorder: its elevation through the day, each hour's height against the day's peak, where now, sunrise and sunset fall,
and how long it is up.

## Today's path

`sunPathDrawing` turns the path into pieces of line for any drawing: the day so far, the day still to come and the
night, the horizon and where the sun is now, all as fractions of the drawing.

<Example file="entities/sun/Path.tsx" />

## In a skin

Orb's `HaSunTile` and Field's `HaSunModule` are `useHaSun` above each skin's card. Each leads with what comes next and
counts down to it, draws today's path (Orb's as a line with the sun on it, Field's as the sun's height by hour), and
opens on every time to come. They take the hook's options as props, plus their own:
