A room's reading: a temperature or humidity `sensor.*`, read with the humidity and battery sensors on the same device. Read-only.

## Quick example

<Example file="entities/sensor/RoomReading.tsx" />

Home Assistant gives each measurement its own entity. The hook reads the companions you name and puts them on one `SensorEntity`, so a card shows the room, not three sensors.

## Component

`<HaSensor>` is `useHaSensor` as a component that draws nothing of its own. It takes every option of the hook as a
prop, and hands the hook's result to its child function. Use it where hooks can't go, such as one per item of a list.

<ApiMembers from="./api-types" name="HaSensorProps" only={["entityId", "children"]} />

## Hook

<ApiSignature from="@dashhaus/core" name="useHaSensor" />

It reads the sensor and its companions, its area, and today's history of the temperature and the humidity companion. `outdoor` marks a sensor outside, where indoor comfort words don't apply.
