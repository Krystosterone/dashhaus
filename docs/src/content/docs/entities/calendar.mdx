One `calendar.*` entity with its events from the start of today to a horizon a week away, and the events of any other
calendars you read beside it, merged into one list. What's next, and when. Read-only.

## Quick example

<Example file="entities/calendar/NextEvent.tsx" />

`calendarAgenda` picks the event a card leads with: one under way, else the next to start. Within a day a timed
event comes before the all-day ones, so bin day never hides the dentist at two. `eventWhen` says when it is, counted
from the entity's own minute: `In 2h 6m`, `Until 12:15`, `All day`, `Tomorrow 09:30`, or on a 12-hour page
`Tomorrow 9:30 AM` (pass it the page's `useHourCycle()`). Every skin says it in these words.

## Component

`<HaCalendar>` is `useHaCalendar` as a component that draws nothing of its own. It takes every option of the hook as
a prop, and hands the hook's result to its child function. Use it where hooks can't go, such as one per item of a
list.

<ApiMembers from="@dashhaus/core" name="HaCalendar" only={["entityId", "children"]} />

## Hook

<ApiSignature from="@dashhaus/core" name="useHaCalendar" />

It reads the calendar and its area, and its events through `calendar.get_events`, one call per calendar, from the start
of today for `days` days. They are read again each hour, on a new day, and whenever the calendar itself changes (an
event starts or ends); the last events stay up while the next read loads. Until the first read settles, the calendar's
own current or next event stands in. Pass `events` to supply your own, or `loadEvents={false}` to skip the read.

#### `history`: CalendarHistory

Today's plan, drawn from the events rather than Home Assistant's recorder: the share of each hour taken by timed events,
the hours still to come included, and the minutes that adds up to. All-day events take no hours. Pass
`loadHistory: false` to leave it out: `history` is then undefined.

## Today's events

`calendarAgenda` also sorts the day: `today` is every event on it, all-day first, those already over kept so the day
reads whole; `left` those not over yet; `later` what starts after today. `eventSpan` says an event's span
(`14:00–15:00`, `All day`).

## In a skin

Orb's `HaCalendarTile` and Field's `HaCalendarModule` are `useHaCalendar` above each skin's card. Each leads with the
next event and when it is, counts what's left today, and opens on today's events and what's coming. With nothing to
the horizon they say Free. They take the hook's options as props, plus their own:
