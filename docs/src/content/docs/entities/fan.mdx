One `fan.*` entity: a tower or desk fan, a ceiling fan, an extractor. On and off, its speed, and the presets,
oscillation and direction it declares.

## Quick example

<Example file="entities/fan/Speed.tsx" />

`speedCount` is how many speeds the fan has, from Home Assistant's `percentage_step`: 3 for a three-speed fan, 100 for a
stepless one. Its speed is always a percentage, so a three-speed fan runs at 33, 67 or 100.

## Component

`<HaFan>` is `useHaFan` as a component that draws nothing of its own. It takes every option of the hook as a prop, and
hands the hook's result to its child function. Use it where hooks can't go, such as one per item of a list.

<ApiMembers from="@dashhaus/core" name="HaFan" only={["entityId", "children"]} />

## Hook

<ApiSignature from="@dashhaus/core" name="useHaFan" />

It reads the fan, its area and today's history.

### Returns

<ApiMembers from="@dashhaus/core" name="HaFanResult" />

#### `entity`: FanEntity

Each `supports…` flag comes from what the fan declares to Home Assistant. Its speed and preset are known only while it
is on; a fan that is off reports neither.

<ApiMembers from="@dashhaus/core/entities/fan/types" name="FanEntity" />

#### `history`: FanHistory

Today, from Home Assistant's recorder, kept up to date by every change since. Pass `history` to supply your own, or
`loadHistory={false}` to skip the read.

<ApiMembers from="@dashhaus/core/entities/fan/types" name="FanHistory" />

#### `actions`: FanHandlers

<ApiMembers from="@dashhaus/core" name="FanHandlers" />

Every action on an unavailable entity is reported to `onActionError` as `unsupported` and never sent.

## Every fan

Hooks can't be called in a loop, so a list renders one headless component per entity. The selector returns a string:
`useHaSelector` re-renders only when its value changes, and a string of ids changes only when an entity comes or goes.
Lists skip each item's history read with `loadHistory={false}`.

## Tasks

### Presets, oscillation and direction

<Example file="entities/fan/Extras.tsx" />

`presetModes` is Home Assistant's `preset_modes`: normal, sleep, nature, whatever the fan has. Direction is a ceiling
fan's: forward for a summer downdraft on most, reverse for winter.

## In a skin

Orb's `HaFanTile` and Field's `HaFanModule` are `useHaFan` above each skin's card. Both set the speed on the light's
groove or fader, one stop per speed, and keep presets, oscillation and direction in its details. They take the hook's
options as props, plus their own:
