One `camera.*` entity: whether it is idle, recording or streaming, and its still, the picture Home Assistant serves for
it. Read-only, with no history.

## Quick example

<Example file="entities/camera/FrontDoor.tsx" />

The still is Home Assistant's own `entity_picture`, a signed address whose token Home Assistant changes every few
minutes; the hook follows it, and gives none while the camera is unavailable, so a past frame never reads as now.
`usePicture` loads it only once its box is on screen and the page is visible, refreshes it only while both stay true,
and keeps the last frame up while the next one loads. Nothing is fetched in the background.

## Component

`<HaCamera>` is `useHaCamera` as a component that draws nothing of its own. It takes every option of the hook as a
prop, and hands the hook's result to its child function. Use it where hooks can't go, such as one per item of a list.

<ApiMembers from="@dashhaus/core" name="HaCamera" only={["entityId", "children"]} />

## Hook

## The live stream

Dashhaus has no stream player of its own. `useHaMoreInfo` opens Home Assistant's own more-info dialog for the camera,
which plays its live stream; outside Home Assistant there is none, and it gives nothing, so open your own details
instead.

<Example file="entities/camera/LiveStream.tsx" />

## In a skin

Orb's `HaCameraTile` and Field's `HaCameraModule` are `useHaCamera` above each skin's card. Each says the state in
words, draws the still beside them (Orb in a quiet well, Field as a window in its screen), says when a still is from
when it stops refreshing, and opens the live stream from the name or the still. A compact row never shows a still. They
take the hook's options as props, plus their own: `refresh`, in seconds, and `paused`, which holds the still until the
card comes back on screen or the panel wakes.
