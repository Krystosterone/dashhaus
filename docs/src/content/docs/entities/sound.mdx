A sound machine's sound: the `switch.*` that plays it, and the volume `number.*` beside it.

## Quick example

<Example file="entities/sound/SoundMachine.tsx" />

Without `volumeEntityId`, the hook looks beside the switch: for `switch.nursery_music` it tries `number.nursery_music_volume`, then `number.nursery_volume`. The volume is a percent of that number's own range.

## Component

`<HaSound>` is `useHaSound` as a component that draws nothing of its own. It takes every option of the hook as a
prop, and hands the hook's result to its child function. Use it where hooks can't go, such as one per item of a list.

<ApiMembers from="@dashhaus/core" name="HaSound" only={["entityId", "children"]} />

## Every sound

A switch doesn't say it plays a sound, so there's nothing in the house to find them by: list them yourself. The Lovelace cards ask for the same thing, with `card: sound` on the switch.
