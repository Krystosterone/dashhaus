## How it looks

Each card has a round button for its main action. Lights, climate and covers get a slider or stepper beside it, and
readings show a big figure. The yellow accent shows what is on, and a dashed outline means a device is unavailable.
Tap a card's name, or press and hold its button, to open more in a sheet. Cards that belong together, like a
nursery's sound machine, night light and white noise, [can join into one](/dashhaus/start/yaml/#bundles-orb).

It follows Home Assistant's light or dark mode, and reads the same on a phone in your hand and a wall panel across the
room: a card never changes height when its state changes, so nothing jumps when a light turns on. The tablet below is
live, on a demo house.

## What it draws

Lights, climate, covers, switches and `input_boolean` modes, sensors, the weather, alarm panels, locks, humidifiers,
fans, sound machines, Owlet socks and guest Wi-Fi. An entity without a card isn't drawn. Every card in every state is
in [Storybook](/dashhaus/storybook/), and [Warnings and alarms](/dashhaus/start/warnings/) shows how Orb tells you
something is wrong.

## Settings

Open the dashboard and press **Edit dashboard** (the pencil, or **⋮**). Its dialog opens on the dashboard's
configuration in YAML; add to it and save:

<Example file="install/orb-dashboard.yaml" title="A wall panel, always dark" />

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `viewing` | `near` (default): a phone or tablet in the hand. `far`: a wall panel read from across the room. |
| `theme` | `home-assistant` (default) follows Home Assistant's light or dark mode; `auto` follows the device; `light` and `dark` hold one. |
| `title` | The title the page prints in its header. `Home` by default. |

To lay out your own cards and rows instead, take control of the dashboard (the same dialog's **⋮ → Take control**),
which keeps Orb's card as it is now, and edit it; [Lay it out in YAML](/dashhaus/start/yaml/) shows how. A dashboard
you take control of no longer lays itself out.

From a terminal or CI, `@dashhaus/orb` and the deploy tool create Orb's dashboard and one of your own rows in one
command: [Deploy from the command line](/dashhaus/start/deploy/). Pick one way: each loads its own copy of Orb.

## If something goes wrong

If **Add dashboard** has no **Orb** under Community dashboards, or the dashboard says `Custom element doesn't exist`
or `Timeout waiting for strategy element`, Home Assistant hasn't loaded Orb yet. Reload the tab. If that doesn't help,
open **Settings → Dashboards → ⋮ → Resources** and check there's a JavaScript module at
`/hacsfiles/orb-dashboard/orb-dashboard.js`.

Community dashboards are listed from Home Assistant 2026.5. On an older version, or one that keeps its dashboards in
YAML, create a dashboard yourself and give it `strategy: { type: custom:orb-dashboard }` as its whole configuration.

## Update

The Marketplace (or HACS) tells you when there's a new version of Orb. Update it there, then reload the tab. Your
dashboard follows; [Release notes](/dashhaus/releases/) say what changed and anything to do first.

## Ask an assistant to do it

An assistant that can use your browser can follow this page on its own. Give it the page's address, or this:

```text
Install the Orb dashboard in my Home Assistant. I'm signed in as an administrator.
1. Open https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/hacs_repository/?owner=Krystosterone&repository=orb-dashboard&category=plugin
   and download Orb (type: Dashboard; add it as a custom repository if asked). Keep the defaults.
2. Reload the browser tab.
3. Settings → Dashboards → Add dashboard → under Community dashboards, pick "Orb" → Create.
4. Open the new Orb dashboard from the sidebar and check it draws.
   Don't change any other dashboard, and never ask me for a password or token.
```
