Orb and Field now install on their own: each has its own download in the Marketplace, and Home Assistant lists it
under its own name in **Add dashboard**. The **Dashhaus** download, which carried both in one dashboard with a tab
for each look, has had its last release. Its dashboards keep working, and each tab says so above it, but it gets no
more updates.

Moving takes a few minutes in your browser, with nothing to install on your computer.

## Move

<Steps>

1. Open the look you use, and download it from its page:

   <LinkButton href="/dashhaus/orb/">Orb</LinkButton> <LinkButton href="/dashhaus/field/" variant="secondary">Field</LinkButton>

   Each page's **Open in Home Assistant** button opens the Marketplace (or HACS before Home Assistant 2026.11) on
   that look; press **Download** and keep the defaults. Use both pages if you use both looks.

2. Reload the browser tab.

3. Open **Settings → Dashboards**, press **Add dashboard**, pick **Orb** or **Field** under **Community
   dashboards**, and press **Create**. It lays your house out as the Dashhaus tab did, and follows that look as it
   updates.

4. Delete the old **Dashhaus** dashboard (**Settings → Dashboards**, open it, **Delete**), then remove the
   **Dashhaus** download in the Marketplace or HACS. Reload the tab once more.

</Steps>

Settings you gave the old dashboard carry over by hand: `viewing`, `theme` (Orb) and `finish` (Field) are the same
keys on the new one (**Edit dashboard**). If you took control of a Dashhaus dashboard and wrote your own rows, those
are the look's card, `custom:dashhaus-orb` or `custom:dashhaus-field`: download the look, keep that dashboard, and
remove Dashhaus. It keeps drawing, now from the look's own file.

<Aside title="Both at once, for a moment">
  Between steps 2 and 4, Home Assistant loads the look twice, once from each download. The first one loaded draws it,
  so nothing breaks; removing Dashhaus in step 4 leaves one.
</Aside>

## Until you move

The Dashhaus dashboard keeps its tabs and its settings. Open it and press **Edit dashboard** to change them:

<Example file="install/strategy.yaml" title="One look, for a wall panel" />

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `skin` | `orb`, `field`, or a list of them in tab order. Every look when left out. |
| `viewing` | `near` (default): a phone or tablet in the hand. `far`: a wall panel read from across the room. |
| `theme` | Orb's colours: `home-assistant` (default, follows its dark mode), `auto`, `light` or `dark`. |
| `finish` | Field's finish: `home-assistant` (default), `auto`, `alloy` or `black`. |
| `title` | The title each page prints in its header. `Home` by default. |

## Ask an assistant to do it

An assistant that can use your browser can follow this page on its own. Give it the page's address, or this:

```text
Move my Home Assistant from the Dashhaus dashboard to Orb. I'm signed in as an administrator.
1. Open https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/hacs_repository/?owner=Krystosterone&repository=orb-dashboard&category=plugin
   and download Orb (type: Dashboard; add it as a custom repository if asked). Keep the defaults.
2. Reload the browser tab.
3. Settings → Dashboards → Add dashboard → under Community dashboards, pick "Orb" → Create. Check it draws.
4. Delete the dashboard named Dashhaus, then remove the Dashhaus download in the Marketplace or HACS, and reload.
   Don't change any other dashboard, and never ask me for a password or token.
```
