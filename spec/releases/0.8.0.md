# Dashhaus 0.8.0

Status: written in its release PR, at the commit whose files [0.8.0.sha256](0.8.0.sha256) records. It
shipped when [v0.8.0](https://github.com/Krystosterone/dashhaus/releases/tag/v0.8.0) exists.

## Release page

Cover cards act by the services a cover declares, and covers, switches, lights, fans and media players whose state
Home Assistant only assumes offer on and off apart in their details. The automatic layouts add your Energy dashboard's
whole-home power and meters, Field's wall fits four rooms on a wide panel, and Orb marks a problem to act on soon.
Breaking for skin builders: the Lovelace schema's `CardShape` is now `ReadCard` (with `readOptions` and `readItem`),
`percentOf` truncates as Home Assistant does, and a toggle's or fan's state can be unknown.

## What changed since 0.7.0

### Breaking changes

- In @dashhaus/core, when the callService you pass createAlarmHandlers rejects an arm or a disarm with something other than an HaActionError, the handler rejects with a sanitized HaActionError instead, keeping only Home Assistant's translation_key and code (as translationKey and providerCode): read those rather than the original error. A wrong code resolves false only when it carries Home Assistant's translation_key "invalid_code", or translationKey on an HaActionError, so reject with one of those to keep the keypad's wrong-code answer.
- In `@dashhaus/core/lovelace/schema`, the read card type `CardShape` is now `ReadCard`, a `CardKindSpec`'s `options` reader is now `readOptions`, and a `LayoutSchema`'s `item` reader is now `readItem`. Rename them in your skin's Lovelace config; `SkinStrategy`'s `options` and a `RowEntry`'s `item` keep their names.
- In `@dashhaus/core`, `percentOf` truncates a speed's percentage as Home Assistant does (`percentOf(2, 3)` is 66, not 67), `useHaFan`'s `onPercentageChange` sends a fractional percentage truncated, and `speedOf` reads a percentage a speed sends exactly as that speed. For the old percentage, use `Math.round((speed / count) * 100)`; for the old speed, `Math.round((percent / 100) * count)`, clamped to 1 to count.
- In @dashhaus/core, ToggleEntity's and FanEntity's state can now be unknown, and mediaPress can answer stop: give an exhaustive switch or a lookup keyed by them the new case.
- Orb's and Field's Lovelace cards now refuse a card whose column and width run past the skin's most columns (Orb 12, Field 4) in a row without columns, where they used to draw that row unanchored: lower its column or its width.
- A cover's asked action is now `CoverAsk` (open, close, or a position): Orb's `CoverCardProps` takes it for `action` and `onOpenDetails`, and Field's `CoverModuleProps` for `defaultAction`. A callback or a value you typed as `CoverAction` should take `CoverAsk`, or narrow it.

### New

- A garage door, door or gate that reports a position can now be moved part way in both skins, and it asks first: a tap on Orb's bar or Field's fader asks straight away, a drag asks when you let go ("Move Front gate to 40%?"), and Cancel puts it back. Its quick positions are Closed and Open. `@dashhaus/core` adds the words for it (`coverQuestion` and `coverAskAction` take a position, `levelQuestion` and `levelAction` for any level that asks).
- @dashhaus/core adds the words a featured card says where its featured content would be while it has none: the media player's `nothingPlayingWords` (No title, Nothing playing) and the vacuum's `NO_CLEANING_HISTORY` (No cleaning history).
- Field's wall layout puts as many rooms side by side as the panel's width holds with every row's name whole across the room: three at 1280 px as before, four on a panel from about 1430 px wide, since a wall's page now takes the whole width instead of stopping at 1240 px.
- `meterRise` from `@dashhaus/core/entities/numeric/readings` adds up how far a meter that only climbs rose over its readings, counting a drop of more than a tenth as a reset from zero, as Home Assistant's statistics do.
- Orb draws a problem to act on soon (a jammed lock, a leak, the entry delay) with a solid warning mark, apart from the outlined one of a problem that can wait. In a compact row that shows a value, such a problem also takes that value's place.
- Orb's and Field's cover cards take a device_class key in their YAML (garage, door or gate), so a garage door Home Assistant gives no class asks first before it opens or closes and warns while open; useHaCover takes it as the deviceClass option.
- The automatic layouts' Energy row in Orb and Field adds your whole home's power and consumption first, and your gas and water meters after the electricity, as you set them up in Home Assistant's Energy dashboard.

### Fixed

- A room sensor reading % with no device class stays a humidity when its YAML sets a temperature unit, instead of also showing the same number as a temperature.
- An energy or water meter's today rises from its reading at midnight rather than its last reading in the first hour, and counts a meter that resets as starting again from zero, as Home Assistant's energy dashboard does. A `NumericHistory` you pass in may carry that rise as the optional `rise`.
- Climate and fan cards in both skins no longer offer a press Home Assistant refuses: a heat-only thermostat, or a fan that doesn't declare turning off, keeps its target and speed but draws no on/off press, and a thermostat with no turn-off service is switched by its modes.
- Climate cards in both skins step a whole degree on a °F thermostat that names no step of its own, as Home Assistant does, instead of half a degree.
- Cover cards in both skins open, close and stop a cover by what it declares: a blind that only tilts moves its slats, slats that only open and close get Closed and Open, and slats stay settable while a cover is unknown.
- Covers, switches, lights, fans and media players whose state Home Assistant only assumes (an RF plug, an IR TV), or doesn't know yet, offer on and off apart in their details in both skins, and an unknown switch or fan says Unknown instead of Unavailable.
- dashhaus-deploy signs in to Home Assistant before copying any file, so a rejected HA_TOKEN leaves the served file matching its resource's version.
- dashhaus-deploy stops with “Home Assistant closed the connection.” when Home Assistant restarts mid-deploy, instead of exiting without a word; if that may have left a dashboard without its layout, the message names it to delete.
- Fan cards in Orb and Field send the percentage Home Assistant itself uses for a speed (66 for speed 2 of 3, not 67), so a fan with 3, 6, 7, 8 or 9 speeds on ESPHome, ZHA or Tuya no longer runs one speed too fast.
- Field's Lovelace card redraws its wall at the new footprint as soon as you add or remove footprint in its YAML, instead of keeping the old one until the house changes.
- Field's wall layout puts three rooms side by side at 1280 px instead of four, so the names in each room's rows read whole from across the room; a row of mixed module sizes that holds rows takes fewer, wider columns for the same reason.
- Media player cards in both skins stop a playing player that can't pause, instead of offering to turn it off.
- Orb's featured cards no longer leave most of the card empty when their featured content is missing: guest Wi-Fi says No QR code where the QR code would be when Home Assistant gives none, a media player that names nothing says Nothing playing or No title, and a vacuum given no history says No cleaning history.
- The sun card's today strip says the clock's hour and draws each hour where the clock has it on a daylight saving day.
- Today's minutes on, armed, asleep, playing and cleaning count from when the state changed, not from the top of its hour, and a climate card's activity is what the unit did longest while it ran; on a daylight saving day they count the day's real 23 or 25 hours.
- Cards in both skins read a temperature unit however Home Assistant writes it: weather cards take "celsius" and "°c" instead of falling back to another unit, climate and sensor cards draw a reading in "℉" as °F instead of °C or the system's unit, and Dyson cards show °F, not a bare °, for a room temperature in "℉" or "fahrenheit".

Internal changes, left out: 2.
